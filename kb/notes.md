# Editing notes

Rules in your own words. The planner reads this file verbatim on every run, so write
the things the numbers cannot carry. One rule per line, optionally scoped to an edit
type in brackets.

Examples (delete these and write your own):

- [talking-head] Never dissolve between two shots of the same speaker; cut or cover with B-roll.
- [brand-film] Open on the widest shot available; hold it at least 3 seconds before the first cut.
- [sports-promo] Hits land on the beat; whooshes lead a cut by 2–3 frames.
- Music never sits above -14 dB under speech.

## Learned from your Diwali Party edit (2026-10-31; observations from one edit, edit freely)

- [event] People first: guests' faces to the camera, posing, laughing, hugging, taking selfies; medium and close shots (87% of shots have a face). Venue wides, crowds from afar and the DJ hardly appear; decor only as 2-3 frame flashes in the intro.
- [event] The brand board (the photo wall with the event's sign) is the motif: arrivals at it, and runs of different guests at the same board, cut fast or stacked one track above the other 0.2 s apart with a click on each (match cuts on the board).
- [event] Card and decor match cuts flicker: 2-3 frames a shot, a click on every cut (six suit shots in 0.56 s).
- [event] Pace: median shot 0.32 s (8 frames), 90% under 0.88 s; a hero moment gets a speed ramp, not a longer hold.
- [event] Ramps: arrivals 100% -> 50% -> 25% (about 0.2 s, 0.6 s, 0.8 s); party shots punch 150% -> 50% -> 125% inside 0.5-1 s.
- [event] Flow: brand flash intro (disco ball, decor, the sign) -> card flicker -> slow-motion arrivals at the board -> montage over the build -> 2 s of black with a riser and a whoosh -> the drop with the fastest cuts and a photo stack -> a short break (casino tables, two still photos, a hit) -> a second song for the finale (prism dance shots) -> a group at the board, slowed, to end.
- [event] Music is a medley from the brief's music folder, joined on drops and hits (Karan Aujla intro, Jogi riff and drop, Rock tha Party finale).
- [event] Sound: a crowd-cheering bed under the party, a riser into each drop, whooshes on section changes, click bursts (4 clicks 0.12-0.2 s apart) on the card flicker and the photo stack, a 2-3 frame flash overlay (sfx Ref/SnapInsta...AQOuV3PDe64) on most cuts.
- [event] Prism-filter shots are wanted (about a third of the finale); the photographer's stills can go in.
